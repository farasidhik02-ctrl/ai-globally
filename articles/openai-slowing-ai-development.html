OpenAI open to slowing AI development as safety concerns grow

By Fara Sidhik · September 11, 2026 · AI Globally

Key Points

  • Altman reportedly told staff OpenAI could pace advanced AI development alongside other frontier labs.
  • OpenAI has already temporarily slowed selected frontier development work while strengthening safeguards.
  • Industry-wide pacing would require coordination among competing labs and regulators.

OpenAI CEO Sam Altman reportedly told employees at a company-wide meeting this week that the company is open to slowing the development of its most advanced AI systems, Bloomberg reported.

Altman said OpenAI could pace development alongside other AI labs, although some competitors may not agree, according to the report. The comments do not amount to an announced company-wide slowdown.

OpenAI has already slowed frontier development

OpenAI has previously put that principle into practice on a limited basis. In August, the company temporarily slowed selected frontier development work while strengthening safeguards, including a two-week pause in reinforcement-learning training for its latest deployable models. The company also held its largest planned frontier RL run before later restarting it with additional safeguards.

Why It Matters

Altman's reported comments move the debate beyond whether individual models are safe enough to release. They raise the prospect of safety thresholds affecting the pace of development itself.

What to Watch

No industry-wide agreement to slow AI development has been announced. The next question is whether frontier AI developers can turn interest in coordination into concrete standards.
