## What is an AI agent?

An AI agent is a software system that uses artificial intelligence to pursue a goal and perform tasks on behalf of a user.

## How are agents different from chatbots?

A chatbot primarily generates responses. An agent can additionally use tools, retrieve information and take actions in external systems when it has permission to do so.

## Why are AI agents important?

The commercial opportunity is shifting from AI that generates information toward AI that can perform useful work.

## What are the risks?

An incorrect agent can do more than produce a bad answer. If it can act on external systems, mistakes may have practical consequences.
