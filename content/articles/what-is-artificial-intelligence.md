## What is artificial intelligence?

Artificial intelligence, or AI, refers broadly to computer systems designed to perform tasks associated with capabilities such as learning, reasoning, prediction, perception, language and decision-making.

## How does AI work?

Much of modern AI is built using machine learning, where models learn patterns from data rather than relying entirely on manually written rules.

## What are large language models?

Large language models, or LLMs, are trained on large amounts of language-related data and can generate text, answer questions, summarise information, translate and write code.

## What are AI agents?

AI agents extend the chatbot model by giving AI systems tools and the ability to carry out tasks.

## What is AGI?

Artificial general intelligence, or AGI, has no universally accepted technical definition and broadly refers to a hypothetical AI capable of performing a wide range of intellectual tasks.
