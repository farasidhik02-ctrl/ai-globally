## There is no single AI model race anymore

The leading AI companies now compete across reasoning, coding, agentic work, multimodality, inference cost, speed, distribution and access.

## OpenAI

OpenAI is pushing toward models that can complete complex end-to-end work and benefits from broad consumer and developer distribution.

## Anthropic

Anthropic has become a major competitor in coding, knowledge work and agentic systems.

## Google DeepMind

Google can combine frontier-model development with distribution across Search, Workspace, Android, Cloud and other products.

## DeepSeek

DeepSeek has made efficiency and cost central to the competition.

## Meta

Meta combines an open-weight model ecosystem with enormous consumer distribution.

## Why It Matters

AI competition is increasingly a fight over the economics and distribution of intelligence, not just raw model performance.
